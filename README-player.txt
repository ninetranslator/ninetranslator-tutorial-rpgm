Nine and the Forest of Words  (ナインとことばの森)
A tiny game for trying ninetranslator - about five minutes long.

It is in Japanese on purpose. To play it in your language:

  1. On a computer, open https://ninetranslator.com/translate/ in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click "Choose folder..." and pick this folder (the one with Game.exe in it).
  3. Pick your language, then click Translate.
  4. Click "Install into the game", then double-click Game.exe.

If you can read the ending, the translation worked.

This game's pictures and story were made for ninetranslator. It runs on the RPG Maker MV
core scripts (MIT licence) and NW.js - see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
